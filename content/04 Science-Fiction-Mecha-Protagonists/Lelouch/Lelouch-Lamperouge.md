## Overview

Lelouch Lamperouge is the main protagonist of _Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion_. Born as Lelouch vi Britannia, he is the exiled prince of the Holy Britannian Empire who seeks to destroy the corrupt empire responsible for his mother's death and his sister Nunnally's suffering. After obtaining the mysterious power known as Geass from the immortal C.C., Lelouch gains the ability to command anyone to obey a single order without question. Using the masked identity of Zero, he leads the Black Knights in a rebellion against Britannia while carefully concealing his true identity from friends, family, and enemies. Although he is not a physically powerful fighter, Lelouch is an extraordinary strategist whose intelligence allows him to defeat opponents with far greater military strength. Throughout the series, he is forced to make difficult moral choices and personal sacrifices in pursuit of a peaceful future. His journey explores the cost of revolution, the burden of leadership, and the consequences of pursuing justice through extreme means.

## Personality

- Brilliant and strategic, always planning several steps ahead of his opponents.
- Charismatic and determined, inspiring others to follow his vision despite overwhelming odds.
- Compassionate beneath his ruthless exterior, especially toward his sister and those closest to him.

## Abilities

1. **Geass (Power of Absolute Obedience)** – Lelouch can issue a single irresistible command to anyone who makes direct eye contact with him, forcing them to obey without question.
2. **Master Tactician** – His exceptional intelligence and military strategy allow him to outmaneuver enemies with superior numbers and resources.
3. **Leadership and Manipulation** – As the leader of the Black Knights, Lelouch unites diverse groups, inspires loyalty, and skillfully manipulates political and military situations to achieve his goals.

### Story Role

Lelouch serves as the central hero of _Code Geass_, driving the series through his rebellion against the Holy Britannian Empire. As Zero, he becomes a symbol of hope for oppressed people while secretly balancing the responsibilities of leading a revolution and protecting those he loves. His actions reshape the political landscape of the world, forcing nations and individuals alike to question justice, freedom, and the true cost of peace. Throughout the story, Lelouch's relationships with C.C., Suzaku Kururugi, Nunnally, and the members of the Black Knights shape both his successes and his failures. His willingness to sacrifice his own reputation and future for the greater good culminates in one of anime's most memorable conclusions, making Lelouch one of the most iconic and complex protagonists in the mecha genre.
