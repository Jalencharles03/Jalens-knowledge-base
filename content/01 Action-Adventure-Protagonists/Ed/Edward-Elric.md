## Overview

Edward Elric is the main protagonist of _Fullmetal Alchemist_ and _Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood_. Known as the "Fullmetal Alchemist," Edward is a gifted young alchemist who earned the title of State Alchemist at an unusually young age. Alongside his younger brother, Alphonse Elric, Edward attempts to use alchemy to bring their deceased mother back to life, violating the forbidden law of human transmutation. The experiment ends in tragedy, costing Edward his left leg and Alphonse his entire body. To save his brother, Edward sacrifices his right arm to bind Alphonse's soul to a suit of armor. After receiving advanced automail prosthetic limbs, Edward dedicates himself to finding the Philosopher's Stone in hopes of restoring their original bodies. Throughout his journey, he uncovers a vast conspiracy involving the Amestrian government, the Homunculi, and the true nature of alchemy. Despite his young age, Edward's intelligence, courage, and determination make him one of the most respected alchemists in the country. His unwavering commitment to protecting others and accepting the consequences of his actions defines his growth throughout the series.

## Personality

- Intelligent and analytical, often solving problems through logic and creativity.
- Determined and resilient, refusing to give up despite overwhelming obstacles.
- Compassionate and protective, especially toward his brother, friends, and innocent people.

## Abilities

1. **Advanced Alchemy** – Edward is an exceptionally skilled alchemist who can perform transmutations without a transmutation circle after opening the Gate of Truth.
2. **Automail Combat** – His mechanical right arm and left leg provide enhanced durability and can be used effectively in hand-to-hand combat and be transmuted into blades and other weapons .
3. **Expert Hand-to-Hand Fighting** – In addition to his alchemy, Edward is a capable martial artist who combines physical combat with transmutations to outmaneuver opponents.

### Story Role

Edward serves as the central hero of _Fullmetal Alchemist_, leading the story through his search to restore his and Alphonse's bodies. His journey explores themes of sacrifice, redemption, morality, and the consequences of seeking forbidden knowledge. As he uncovers the truth behind the Philosopher's Stone and the Homunculi, Edward becomes a key figure in preventing a nationwide catastrophe that threatens the entire country of Amestris. His relationship with Alphonse is the emotional core of the series, demonstrating the strength of family, loyalty, and selflessness. Through his growth as both an alchemist and a person, Edward learns that true strength comes not from power alone but from accepting responsibility, valuing human life, and relying on those he trusts. His character has become one of the most respected and iconic protagonists in anime and manga.
