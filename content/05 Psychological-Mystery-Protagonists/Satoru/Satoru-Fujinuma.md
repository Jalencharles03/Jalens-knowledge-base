## Overview

Satoru Fujinuma is the main protagonist of _Erased_ (_Boku dake ga Inai Machi_) and a struggling manga artist who works part-time as a pizza delivery driver while pursuing his dream of becoming successful. He possesses a mysterious ability called "Revival," which automatically sends him a few minutes back in time whenever a life-threatening incident is about to occur, giving him the opportunity to prevent tragedies. After his mother is murdered and he is falsely accused of the crime, Satoru is unexpectedly sent eighteen years into the past to his childhood. Realizing that the murder is connected to a series of child kidnappings and killings that occurred during his elementary school years, he sets out to prevent the crimes before they happen. Returning to his younger body but retaining his adult memories, Satoru must earn the trust of his classmates while uncovering the identity of the true culprit. Throughout the series, he demonstrates remarkable courage and selflessness, risking everything to save innocent lives. His journey explores themes of redemption, friendship, responsibility, and the lasting impact that small acts of kindness can have on others.

## Personality

- Compassionate and selfless, always putting the safety of others before his own.
- Intelligent and observant, carefully analyzing situations to uncover the truth.
- Determined and resilient, refusing to give up even when faced with overwhelming challenges.

## Abilities

1. **Revival** – Satoru's mysterious ability allows him to travel back in time before accidents or tragedies occur, giving him the chance to prevent them.
2. **Deductive Reasoning** – He possesses strong observational and analytical skills, enabling him to notice important details and solve difficult mysteries.
3. **Perseverance** – Although he has no supernatural combat abilities, Satoru repeatedly overcomes obstacles through determination, courage, and careful planning.

### Story Role

Satoru serves as the central hero of _Erased_, with his journey revolving around preventing a series of tragic murders and uncovering the truth behind the events that shaped his childhood. His return to the past allows him to build meaningful friendships with classmates, particularly Kayo Hinazuki, whose fate becomes central to his mission. Rather than relying on physical strength, Satoru succeeds through empathy, intelligence, and an unwavering determination to protect those who cannot protect themselves. As he pieces together the mystery surrounding the murders, he also grows emotionally, learning to confront his own regrets and appreciate the importance of human connection. Through its blend of suspense, time travel, and heartfelt storytelling, Satoru's journey makes _Erased_ one of anime's most memorable mystery series and establishes him as a compelling and deeply relatable protagonist.
