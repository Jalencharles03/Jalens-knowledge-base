## Overview

Dr. Kenzo Tenma is the main protagonist of _Monster_ and a brilliant Japanese neurosurgeon working in Germany. At the beginning of the series, Tenma is highly respected for his medical talent and is on the path to a prestigious career. However, his life changes forever when he chooses to save the life of a young boy named Johan Liebert instead of following orders to operate on a politically influential patient. Although Tenma believes he has acted according to his duty as a doctor, he later discovers that Johan has grown into a ruthless serial killer responsible for countless deaths. Haunted by guilt and determined to stop the monster he unknowingly saved, Tenma abandons his successful career and embarks on a dangerous journey across Europe to find Johan. Along the way, he encounters detectives, criminals, and ordinary people whose lives have all been touched by Johan's actions. Despite facing constant danger and impossible moral dilemmas, Tenma refuses to abandon his principles, believing that every human life has equal value. His journey explores justice, morality, responsibility, and the consequences of one's choices.

## Personality

- Compassionate and selfless, believing every patient deserves the same chance to live.
- Intelligent and resourceful, using his medical knowledge and critical thinking to overcome difficult situations.
- Principled and resilient, remaining true to his moral values even when faced with overwhelming hardship.

## Abilities

1. **Expert Neurosurgeon** – Tenma is a world-class brain surgeon whose exceptional medical skill earns him international recognition and allows him to save lives in the most difficult circumstances.
2. **Analytical Thinking** – He carefully examines evidence, observes details, and pieces together information to track Johan and uncover the truth behind a series of murders.
3. **Survival and Adaptability** – Although not a trained fighter, Tenma learns to survive while on the run, adapting to dangerous situations and making difficult decisions under pressure.

### Story Role

Kenzo Tenma serves as the central hero of _Monster_, driving the story through his pursuit of Johan Liebert and his search for redemption. His decision to save Johan's life sets every major event of the series into motion, forcing him to confront the devastating consequences of his actions. Throughout his journey, Tenma is repeatedly challenged to question whether taking a life can ever be justified, even if it means preventing countless future deaths. His encounters with Johan, Inspector Lunge, Nina Fortner, and many other characters explore themes of good and evil, justice, free will, and the value of human life. Rather than relying on physical strength or supernatural abilities, Tenma's greatest weapons are his compassion, integrity, and unwavering commitment to doing what he believes is right, making him one of anime's most compelling and morally grounded protagonists.
