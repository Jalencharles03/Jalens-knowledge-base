## Overview

Guts is the main protagonist of _Berserk_ and one of the most iconic characters in dark fantasy manga and anime. Born from tragedy and raised as a mercenary, Guts spends his childhood surviving brutal battles and learning to rely only on his own strength. His life changes when he joins the Band of the Hawk, a renowned mercenary group led by the ambitious Griffith. There, Guts forms meaningful friendships and begins to discover a sense of belonging for the first time. However, a devastating betrayal during the Eclipse forever alters his life, leaving him physically and emotionally scarred. Branded with the Mark of Sacrifice, Guts is relentlessly pursued by demonic creatures while seeking revenge against Griffith and the God Hand. Armed with the massive Dragonslayer sword and an unbreakable will, Guts battles both supernatural enemies and his own inner darkness. Throughout the series, he gradually learns to trust others again and finds new companions who help him carry the burdens of his painful past. His journey explores themes of survival, revenge, perseverance, and the struggle to maintain one's humanity in a cruel world.

## Personality

- Determined and resilient, refusing to give up regardless of the hardships he faces.
- Protective and loyal, risking everything to keep his companions safe.
- Stoic and independent, though he gradually learns the importance of trusting others.

## Abilities

1. **Master Swordsman** – Guts wields the enormous Dragonslayer sword with incredible skill, strength, and precision, allowing him to defeat both human and supernatural opponents.
2. **Berserker Armor** – The Berserker Armor dramatically enhances Guts' physical abilities, enabling him to fight beyond his normal limits while placing immense strain on his body and mind.
3. **Exceptional Combat Experience** – Years of constant warfare have made Guts an expert in swordsmanship, survival, strategy, and hand-to-hand combat against overwhelming odds.

### Story Role

Guts serves as the central hero of _Berserk_, with his journey portraying one of the most compelling stories of perseverance in manga. His quest for revenge against Griffith drives much of the narrative, but his true growth comes from learning to move beyond hatred and protect the people who become his new family. Throughout the series, Guts confronts terrifying Apostles, supernatural horrors, and the overwhelming influence of fate itself while refusing to surrender his free will. His relationships with Casca, Griffith, Farnese, Schierke, Serpico, and Isidro shape his development from a lone warrior into a reluctant leader who learns to rely on others. Through his relentless determination and refusal to be broken by unimaginable suffering, Guts embodies the themes of resilience, sacrifice, and the enduring struggle to preserve one's humanity, making him one of the greatest protagonists in manga history.
