## Overview

Shigeo Kageyama, better known by his nickname "Mob," is the main protagonist of _Mob Psycho 100_. He is a quiet and socially awkward middle school student who possesses some of the strongest psychic powers in the world. Despite his incredible abilities, Mob believes that psychic powers alone do not determine a person's worth and strives to improve himself through ordinary means. He works part-time for the self-proclaimed psychic Reigen Arataka, who often relies on Mob's powers to solve supernatural cases while teaching him valuable life lessons. Throughout the series, Mob encounters powerful espers, evil spirits, and dangerous organizations that seek to exploit his abilities. Rather than pursuing power, Mob's greatest goal is to become a kinder, more confident person and to express his emotions in a healthy way. His psychic abilities grow stronger as his emotions build, eventually reaching a breaking point represented by his "100%" state. Through his friendships, personal struggles, and emotional growth, Mob learns that true strength comes from compassion, self-improvement, and accepting who he is.

## Personality

- Humble and kind, treating others with respect regardless of their abilities.
- Quiet and reserved, often keeping his emotions hidden beneath a calm exterior.
- Determined to improve himself through hard work rather than relying solely on his psychic powers.

## Abilities

1. **Immense Psychic Power** – Mob possesses extraordinary telekinetic and psychic abilities that allow him to battle powerful espers and evil spirits with ease.
2. **Emotion-Based Power Growth** – His psychic strength increases dramatically as his emotions build, reaching overwhelming levels when they hit 100% or beyond.
3. **Spirit Exorcism and Energy Manipulation** – Mob can exorcise powerful spirits, create psychic barriers, manipulate energy, and perform a wide variety of advanced psychic techniques.

### Story Role

Mob serves as the central hero of _Mob Psycho 100_, with his journey focusing more on emotional maturity than becoming stronger in battle. Although he is one of the most powerful psychics in the series, he constantly strives to live an ordinary life and build meaningful relationships with others. Throughout the story, Mob confronts dangerous supernatural threats while learning valuable lessons about confidence, empathy, and self-worth. His relationship with Reigen, his friendships with classmates, and his rivalry with other espers all contribute to his personal development. By choosing kindness over violence whenever possible, Mob demonstrates that true strength lies in emotional resilience, humility, and the willingness to grow as a person, making him one of anime's most unique and inspiring protagonists.
