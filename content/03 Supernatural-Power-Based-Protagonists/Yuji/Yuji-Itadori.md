## Overview

Yuji Itadori is the main protagonist of _Jujutsu Kaisen_ and a high school student whose life changes dramatically after he swallows one of Ryomen Sukuna's cursed fingers to save his friends. By doing so, Yuji becomes the vessel for Sukuna, one of the most powerful and dangerous curses in history. Instead of being immediately executed, he is accepted into Tokyo Jujutsu High, where he trains under Satoru Gojo to become a jujutsu sorcerer while searching for the remaining cursed fingers. Throughout the series, Yuji battles powerful curses alongside classmates Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki, growing stronger through intense training and difficult experiences. Although he possesses remarkable physical abilities even without cursed energy, Yuji is constantly challenged by the emotional burden of witnessing death and the responsibility of containing Sukuna. Despite these hardships, he remains determined to protect innocent people and give others a proper death. His journey explores themes of sacrifice, responsibility, and finding purpose in the face of overwhelming tragedy.

## Personality

- Compassionate and selfless, always putting the safety of others before himself.
- Courageous and determined, refusing to give up even against overwhelming odds.
- Honest and empathetic, treating both friends and strangers with kindness and respect.

## Abilities

1. **Superhuman Physical Strength** – Even before becoming a jujutsu sorcerer, Yuji possessed extraordinary speed, strength, agility, and endurance that far exceeded those of an average human.
2. **Cursed Energy Control** – Through his training at Jujutsu High, Yuji learns to channel cursed energy into powerful attacks, including techniques such as Divergent Fist and Black Flash.
3. **Sukuna's Vessel** – As the host of Ryomen Sukuna, Yuji can withstand the immense power of the King of Curses, granting him exceptional durability and the potential for tremendous growth.

### Story Role

Yuji serves as the central hero of _Jujutsu Kaisen_, with his decision to consume Sukuna's cursed finger setting the events of the story into motion. His role revolves around protecting humanity from curses while collecting Sukuna's remaining fingers, knowing that doing so will eventually lead to his own execution. Throughout the series, Yuji faces increasingly powerful enemies and heartbreaking losses that force him to question the meaning of life, death, and justice. His relationships with mentors like Satoru Gojo and friends such as Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki shape his growth into a capable jujutsu sorcerer. Through his courage, compassion, and determination to save others despite his tragic circumstances, Yuji embodies the themes of sacrifice, resilience, and the value of every human life, making him one of the defining protagonists of modern shonen anime.
