## Overview

Inuyasha is the main protagonist of _Inuyasha_ and a half-demon (hanyo) born from the union of the powerful dog demon Toga and the human woman Izayoi. Because of his mixed heritage, Inuyasha is rejected by both humans and demons, leaving him lonely and determined to prove his strength. His life changes when he meets Kagome Higurashi, a modern-day high school student who is transported to Japan's Sengoku period through the Bone-Eater's Well. Together, they embark on a journey to recover the shattered pieces of the Sacred Jewel before they fall into the hands of the evil half-demon Naraku. Along the way, they are joined by loyal companions including Miroku, Sango, Shippo, and Kirara. Throughout the series, Inuyasha struggles with his identity, his lingering feelings for Kikyo, and his desire to become stronger while remaining true to himself. As he matures, he learns to trust others, embrace both sides of his heritage, and fight not for power alone but to protect those he loves. His journey is one of self-acceptance, friendship, and overcoming prejudice.

## Personality

- Brave and determined, always willing to face dangerous enemies head-on.
- Protective and loyal, putting the safety of his friends above his own.
- Hot-headed and stubborn, but deeply compassionate beneath his tough exterior.

## Abilities

1. **Tessaiga** – Inuyasha wields the legendary sword Tessaiga, which grants him powerful techniques capable of defeating demons and protecting humans.
2. **Half-Demon Strength** – His supernatural heritage gives him enhanced strength, speed, agility, endurance, and powerful senses that surpass those of ordinary humans.
3. **Demonic Transformations** – Under certain conditions, Inuyasha can unleash his full demon instincts, dramatically increasing his power while risking the loss of control over himself.

### Story Role

Inuyasha serves as the central hero of _Inuyasha_, leading the quest to recover the Sacred Jewel shards and defeat Naraku before he can corrupt the world with their power. His journey explores themes of identity, belonging, forgiveness, and the struggle between one's human and demonic nature. Throughout the series, Inuyasha grows from a lonely warrior who trusts no one into a dependable leader who values friendship and teamwork. His relationships with Kagome, Miroku, Sango, Shippo, and Kikyo shape both his emotional development and his decisions throughout the story. By embracing both his human heart and his demonic strength, Inuyasha becomes a symbol of perseverance and acceptance, making him one of the most beloved protagonists in fantasy anime.
