## Overview

Aladdin is one of the main protagonists of _Magi: The Labyrinth of Magic_ and a mysterious young magician with extraordinary magical abilities. At the beginning of the series, he travels the world to learn about humanity while searching for the purpose of his existence. Along the way, he befriends Alibaba Saluja and Morgiana, and together they conquer dangerous dungeons, uncover ancient secrets, and challenge powerful enemies. Aladdin is later revealed to be a Magi, a rare magician chosen to guide kings and help shape the future of the world. As the story progresses, he studies at the Magnostadt Academy to improve his magic and gains a deeper understanding of the conflicts between nations, races, and political powers. Although he is kindhearted and innocent, Aladdin demonstrates remarkable wisdom for his age and consistently seeks peaceful solutions whenever possible. His journey revolves around understanding the world, helping others realize their potential, and preventing those who seek to manipulate history for their own ambitions.

## Personality

- Kind and compassionate, always willing to help those in need.
- Curious and open-minded, eager to learn about different people and cultures.
- Wise and optimistic, believing that cooperation and understanding can overcome conflict.

## Abilities

1. **Magic Mastery** – As a Magi, Aladdin possesses enormous amounts of Magoi and can cast powerful spells using his flute and metal vessel.
2. **Ugo** – Aladdin can summon Ugo, a powerful giant with incredible strength who protects him in battle and helps overcome powerful enemies.
3. **Rukh Manipulation** – He can communicate with and manipulate the Rukh, allowing him to perform advanced magic and sense the flow of destiny.

### Story Role

Aladdin serves as one of the central protagonists of _Magi: The Labyrinth of Magic_ and acts as the heart of the story. As a Magi, he guides future kings and encourages leaders to create a world built on freedom, equality, and cooperation rather than fear and oppression. His friendship with Alibaba and Morgiana forms the foundation of the series, and together they confront corrupt rulers, powerful organizations, and ancient threats that seek to control humanity's future. Throughout the story, Aladdin's wisdom, compassion, and unwavering belief in people inspire those around him to become better leaders and individuals. His journey highlights themes of friendship, destiny, responsibility, and the importance of using knowledge and power to improve the lives of others, making him one of the defining characters in the _Magi_ series.
