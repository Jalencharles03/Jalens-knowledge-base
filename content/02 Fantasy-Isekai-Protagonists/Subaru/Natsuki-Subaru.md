## Overview

Subaru Natsuki is the main protagonist of _Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World_. He is an ordinary teenager from modern Japan who is suddenly transported to the fantasy world of Lugunica. Unlike many heroes in other fantasy stories, Subaru does not receive overwhelming strength or special combat abilities when he arrives. Instead, he gains a mysterious ability called Return by Death, which allows him to return to a previous point in time after experiencing death. While this power gives him opportunities to change tragic outcomes, it also forces him to endure difficult experiences and carry the emotional burden of events that others cannot remember. Throughout his journey, Subaru becomes involved with Emilia, Rem, Ram, and many others as he attempts to protect them from dangerous threats and uncover the mysteries surrounding his ability. Although Subaru begins as an impulsive and insecure person, his determination, empathy, and willingness to face his weaknesses allow him to grow into a dependable leader. His story focuses on overcoming fear, accepting personal flaws, and finding the strength to continue moving forward despite impossible challenges.

## Personality

- Determined and persistent, refusing to abandon others even when situations seem hopeless.
- Compassionate and empathetic, deeply caring about the struggles of those around him.
- Emotional and self-reflective, constantly learning from his failures and mistakes.

## Abilities

1. **Return by Death** – Subaru's mysterious ability allows him to travel back to a previous point in time after dying, giving him the chance to change future events.
2. **Strategic Thinking** – Despite lacking overwhelming physical power, Subaru uses observation, planning, and knowledge gained from previous lives and experiences to overcome stronger opponents.
3. **Spirit Arts & Authority of Sloth** – Subaru develops his ability to work with spirits and later gains the Authority of Sloth, which provides him with additional supernatural abilities.

### Story Role

Subaru serves as the central hero of _Re:Zero_, with the story focusing on his personal growth rather than traditional physical strength. His journey challenges the idea of what makes a hero by showing that courage, perseverance, and emotional strength are just as important as combat ability. Throughout the series, Subaru repeatedly faces impossible situations involving powerful enemies, political conflicts, and supernatural mysteries while trying to protect Emilia and his friends. His relationships with characters such as Emilia, Rem, and Beatrice are central to his development, helping him become more confident and understanding. Through his struggles, Subaru learns the importance of accepting help, valuing his own life, and continuing forward despite failure, making him a unique and memorable protagonist in the isekai genre.
