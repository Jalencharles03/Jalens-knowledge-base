## Overview

Kirito, whose real name is Kazuto Kirigaya, is the main protagonist of _Sword Art Online_. He is a highly skilled gamer who becomes trapped inside the virtual reality game Sword Art Online along with thousands of other players. When the game's creator announces that death in the game will result in death in the real world, Kirito is forced to fight for survival while helping clear the game's one hundred floors. As a former beta tester, he possesses valuable knowledge that allows him to progress quickly, though this often causes him to isolate himself from other players. Throughout the series, Kirito participates in multiple virtual worlds, including Alfheim Online, Gun Gale Online, and the Underworld. Along the way, he forms deep friendships and develops a strong relationship with Asuna Yuuki, who becomes one of the most important people in his life. Despite his exceptional abilities, Kirito constantly struggles with the responsibility of protecting others and the emotional weight of past losses. His journey focuses on courage, friendship, and the impact that technology can have on human lives and relationships.

## Personality

- Brave and determined, willing to face dangerous situations to protect others.
- Loyal and compassionate, deeply caring for his friends and loved ones.
- Intelligent and adaptable, capable of quickly analyzing situations and finding solutions.

## Abilities

1. **Dual-Wielding** – Kirito possesses the unique Dual Blades skill, allowing him to wield two swords simultaneously and unleash powerful combinations.
2. **Master Swordsman** – He is an exceptionally skilled fighter whose speed, reflexes, and combat instincts make him one of the strongest players in multiple virtual worlds.
3. **Adaptability in Virtual Worlds** – Kirito quickly masters new game systems, technologies, and combat styles, allowing him to excel across different virtual environments.

### Story Role

Kirito serves as the central hero of _Sword Art Online_ and acts as the primary link between the series' various virtual worlds and story arcs. His actions help save countless players trapped in deadly games and expose the dangers of individuals who misuse advanced technology. Throughout the series, Kirito repeatedly confronts powerful opponents, criminal organizations, and artificial intelligence-related threats that endanger both virtual and real-world lives. His relationship with Asuna and his friendships with other players provide much of the emotional core of the story. As he navigates increasingly advanced virtual realities, Kirito explores themes of identity, responsibility, technology, and the meaning of human connection. His courage and determination make him one of the most recognizable protagonists in modern anime.
